The car in `car.webp`, `car-sm.webp`, `wheel-front.webp` and `wheel-rear.webp` is made from:

**"McLaren MP4-26"** by **Gil Abrantes**, taken at pre-season testing, Circuit de Barcelona-Catalunya, 21 February 2011.
Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:McLaren_MP4-26.jpg
Licence: [CC BY 2.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/)

Changes made: background removed, edges cleaned, cropped, resized and converted to WebP; the two wheel
discs were cut out separately so they can be rotated on top of the static car (see `scripts/make_car.py`). `car-xs.webp` is a smaller export used on the contribution circuit, and
`car-drive.gif` / `car-spin.gif` are looping GIF renders of the same cut-out (see `scripts/make_gif.py`).

The car, its livery and sponsor logos are the property of their respective owners and appear here only
as an illustration on a personal profile.
